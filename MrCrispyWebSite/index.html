Our Story

Mr. Crispy started as a small dream to bring the authentic taste of Indian street food to the people of Rourkela. What began as a single kiosk has now grown into two beloved locations, each serving the same quality and passion that started it all.

Our secret lies in using fresh ingredients, traditional recipes, and a lot of love. From our signature potato twisters to mouth-watering momos, every dish is crafted to perfection by our experienced team.

We believe that great food brings people together, and that's exactly what we strive to do every day - create memorable experiences one crispy bite at a time.
